REAL WEBRTC · NOISE_IK ENCRYPTED · ZERO-KNOWLEDGE SIGNALING

A real video call, actually encrypted.

Enter your e-mail to go online, then call anyone else who's online by theirs. Two browser tabs join a real Agent-Fabric channel, run a genuine Noise_IK handshake, and exchange encrypted WebRTC signaling to open a real RTCPeerConnection — the operator never sees your audio, video, or chat text, at any point. (It does learn who's calling whom, the same way a phone exchange routes a call without hearing it — see "what's real" alongside.)

Pair a new device to this same identity (so it gets your contacts and chat history too, instead of starting empty) -- open this app on the new device, choose "pair from another device" there, then enter the code it shows you here.
